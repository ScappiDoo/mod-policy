Which mods you can use here

Every mod we check for, what it does, and whether you can use it. If your mod is not on this list it is probably fine. If it does something you can see on this list, ask staff first and save yourself the trouble.

What the three labels mean

Not allowed
Take it out of your mods folder before you log in. These do something the game does not let you do, or they play for you while you are away from the keyboard.
Ask first
Some of these are fine here and some are not, and a few are fine once you turn one setting off. Ask before you use one. Nobody gets in trouble for asking.
Allowed
Use them. Most people here already run a few of these and nobody minds.

The number beside each row is how much weight that mod carries if a check finds it. A high number is not a ban and a low number is not permission. It only decides how closely somebody looks.

List updated 10 September 2026. Mods change between versions, so if something here looks wrong or your mod is missing, tell staff and we will check it.

Finding a mod is not the same as catching somebody using it. Nothing happens automatically. A person looks at every case before anything is done.
